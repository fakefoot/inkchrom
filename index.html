Case brief

Three suspects. One pen wrote the note.

A note was left at the scene. Each suspect was carrying a black ballpoint pen. Black ink is a mixture of coloured dyes — separate them on chromatography paper and match the note to the pen that wrote it.

Spot the note ink and each pen on its own strip, run the solvent, then measure the bands and compare Rf values and colours.
